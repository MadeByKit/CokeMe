# CokeMe 🥤

**CokeMe** is a modern Coca-Cola product recommender app designed to help users discover the perfect beverage based on their personal preferences and location. Built for Coca-Cola fans and curious drinkers alike, the app makes it easy to explore Coca-Cola’s extensive product lineup—from refreshing fruit drinks to low- or no-sugar options and energy-boosting beverages.this also gives a feedback to coca-cola to know where certain products are getting requested from so as to provide the product to the country.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Personalized Recommendations**
  Get tailored drink suggestions using preference tags such as *Fruity*, *Low/No Sugar*, *Energy*, and *Immunity Support*.

* **Country-Based Filtering**
  View only products available in your selected country or region.

* **A feedback system on what people from a certain country want.**
  For example, if you find a product you want but isnt available in your country, you can press the request button to send request to coca-cola, this helps them know what people want, especially if its highly demanded.

* **Smart Matching System**
  Results are intelligently grouped into:

  * **Perfect Matches** – products that meet all selected criteria
  * **Partial Matches** – products that meet some of your preferences

* **Engaging Visual Experience**
  A premium, modern UI featuring a dynamic video background and smooth animations for an enjoyable browsing experience.

* **Intuitive & Responsive UI**
  Simple navigation and responsive design make discovering new drinks fast and fun on any device.
